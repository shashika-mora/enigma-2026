### Questions & Answers

#### General
**🤔 What is Enigma?**
Enigma is a mathematics and computer science competition organized by the Mathematics Society of University of Moratuwa. It features challenging problems that require analytical thinking and problem-solving skills, inspired by detective work.

#### Teams
**👥 Team Requirements?**
Teams can have 1-4 members. Both solo participants and teams are welcome. Mixed teams from different universities are encouraged!

**🔄 Can I Change Teams?**
Team changes are allowed until the registration deadline (February 25, 2025). Contact us via email for team modifications.

#### Eligibility
**🎓 Who Can Participate?**
Open to all university students, recent graduates, and professionals. No specific prerequisites required - just bring your problem-solving enthusiasm!

#### Competition
**🧩 What Types of Problems?**
Mathematical puzzles, algorithmic challenges, logical reasoning problems, and computational thinking tasks. Think Sherlock Holmes meets computer science!

**⏱️ Competition Format?**
Full-day competition (8 AM - 6 PM) with multiple rounds including individual challenges, team problems, and final presentations. Breaks and meals provided.

#### Prizes
**🏆 What Can I Win?**
Cash prizes, certificates, networking opportunities, and the honor of being crowned Enigma champions! Plus valuable experience in competitive problem-solving.

#### Preparation
**📱 What Should I Bring?**
Laptop, calculator, writing materials, and your detective mindset! Power banks recommended. Meals and refreshments will be provided.

**🔧 Technical Requirements?**
Bring your own laptop with preferred programming languages/tools installed. WiFi and power outlets will be provided. No specific software requirements.

#### Logistics
**📍 Where & When?**
University of Moratuwa campus on March 15, 2025. Detailed venue information will be sent to registered participants via email.

**🚗 Parking & Transportation?**
Free parking is available on campus. Public transportation options and ride-sharing coordinates will be shared with registered participants.

#### Registration
**💰 Registration Fees?**
Student rate: LKR 500 per member, Team package: LKR 1500 for 3-4 members, Professional rate: LKR 800 per individual. All rates include meals and materials.
